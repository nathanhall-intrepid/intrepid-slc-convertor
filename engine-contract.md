# STL engine contract

All mesh coordinates are millimetres. STL itself is unitless: `parseSTL(buffer,name,{unitScale:1,onProgress})` uses an explicit scale. Binary files with `solid` headers are detected by their exact record length. ASCII and binary inputs return `{name,positions:Float64Array,triangleCount,bounds:{min,max,size,center},format,unitScale}`. Parsing reports triangle progress every 4,096 facets.

`analyzeMesh(mesh,onProgress)` returns manifold/open-edge and winding diagnostics, signed/absolute volume, surface area and bounds. It uses exact shared-vertex identity, consistently with slicing; it never merges nearby geometry merely for diagnostics (`weldToleranceMm:0`). Results and vertex indexes cache on the mesh. `transformMesh(mesh,{rotation:[xDeg,yDeg,zDeg],translation:[x,y,z],ground:true})` creates a transformed copy; it clears cached geometry, topology and analysis. `ground` moves the transformed mesh's lowest Z to zero.

`sliceAt(meshes,zMm,{validate:true,recover:true,zBounds})` returns `{rings,depths,outerRings,areaMm2,outerMaxNormalZ,vertexCount,toleranceMm,sampleZMm,recovery}`. Rings omit their closing duplicate. Exteriors are CCW; holes CW. Geometry is intersected using canonical mesh-edge identities and a consistent interpolation direction, rather than proximity-merging unrelated endpoints. Positive-length segments are retained even when shorter than the old weld tolerance. Closed nonzero-area contours are never discarded based on model size. Translation-stable signed area avoids cancellation far from the origin. Open junctions, self intersections and conflicting source facet orientation are checked. A globally reversed closed mesh is accepted. Mixed winding or nested overlapping positive solids must be repaired; the tool does not guess which are voids. Separate placed parts may not touch or overlap.

Recovery only applies to a singular vertex/edge sampling plane on an exactly closed, consistently wound mesh. Its numerical offset is at most 0.00001 mm, bounded by adjacent distinct vertex elevations and optional `zBounds:[bottom,top]`. Both preview and export use this same sampler. The returned `recovery` is `{shiftedPlane,shiftMm,reason?}` and `sampleZMm` is the actual plane. Real open boundaries never qualify for this sampling retry.

`repairMesh(mesh,options,onProgress)` returns `{mesh,report}` without mutating source positions. It removes exact same-orientation duplicate facets and true zero-area facets. Optional `maxGapMm` defaults to the model diagonal × 0.000001, bounded to 0.000001–0.001 mm; zero disables seam stitching. Values above 0.001 mm are rejected. A seam can be stitched only when two whole simple boundary loops match uniquely with opposite edge orientation, have disjoint endpoints, and already belong to the same connected surface. Each matched edge must be more than 20 times the tolerance long. The resulting topology must introduce no degenerate facets, non-manifold edges, or winding conflicts. Closed parts, disconnected shells, partial/ambiguous seams, and missing-face boundary loops are not welded. No new faces or hole-filling caps are created. This repairs numerical seams; it is not general-purpose STL repair.

The repair report contains `{changed,weldedVertices,removedDegenerateTriangles,removedDuplicateTriangles,maxVertexShiftMm,seamToleranceMm,boundaryEdgesBefore,boundaryEdgesAfter,nonManifoldEdgesAfter,inconsistentEdgesAfter,watertight,warnings}`. Unresolved open/non-manifold/winding defects remain explicit and prevent export. Preserve this report in the job diagnostics so any geometric displacement is reviewable.

`planLayers(meshes,options,onProgress)` supports:

- `baselineUm:100`, `preferredUm:150`; integer values from 50 through 200.
- `supportEndMm:0`, explicitly supplied by the UI/user. This marks a region; it does not create supports.
- `overrides:[{id,fromMm,toMm,thicknessUm}]`; overlapping overrides are rejected.
- `verticalToleranceDeg:3`, `automatic:true`.

It returns `{layers,heightMm,heightUm,actualHeightMm,baselineUm,preferredUm,supportEndMm,overrides,warnings,classificationSamples,maskConvention}`. Each printable layer is `{index,zMm,zStartMm,thicknessUm,requestedUm,reason,classification,adjusted}`. `reason` is support, manual, vertical or standard. There is no artificial Z=0 anchor. `zMm` is the interval **end**. The SLC writer converts this to its minimum-Z record.

Height-range boundaries snap to 50 µm increments, with reported warnings. The final mesh height rounds to the nearest micron (at most 0.5 µm difference). All actual layers remain integer 50–200 µm. Terminal steps may differ from the requested thickness to fit exact section endpoints; `adjusted` marks them. A 51 µm-high section cannot physically be represented as exact 50 µm layers without truncation, so it gets one 51 µm interval.

Automatic fast layers are permitted when the **outermost** slice boundaries remain vertical to the angle tolerance. Facet slopes are evaluated on slice segments at near-endpoints and in intervals between relevant vertex Z events. Dense internal events are excluded only when their complete XY triangle projections lie strictly inside a vertical outer enclosure whose contributing facets span the whole candidate layer interval. This prevents a temporarily hidden exterior shoulder from being mistaken for internal geometry. Exterior horizontal caps and ledges inside a candidate interval explicitly retain baseline layers. More than 24 remaining distinct candidate events conservatively retains baseline and produces a warning; densely tessellated exterior walls or near-vertical enclosures that cannot be proved constant can therefore yield less speed-up.

Nested internal contours do not drive the decision; STL cannot semantically distinguish lattice from critical internal cavities, and the returned warning says so. The actual sliced masks always preserve internal contours; their exclusion applies only to automatic thickness selection. Support and manual bands intentionally override automatic criteria.

`sliceBuild(meshes,plan,onProgress)` performs actual slicing and adds `rings,areaMm2,sampleZMm,recovery` to each layer. Masks are sampled at each interval midpoint through the shared `sliceAt` routine, with that layer's bounds. It checks watertightness and consistent facet winding before export and reports any recovered singular planes through `recoveredPlanes`, `maxPlaneShiftMm`, and a warning. Layer endpoints are unchanged.

Callbacks emit `{stage:'planning'|'slicing'|'parsing',current,total,percent}` or `{stage:'analyzing'|'repairing',percent,message}` synchronously, suitable for posting from a web worker. Repair includes progress during its initial and final topology checks. Cancel by terminating the worker. Browser limits: 200 MB STL, 20,000 layers and 3 million exported contour vertices. There is no hard triangle-count cap; browser memory and processing time still constrain complex files. `createBoxMesh` and `createDemoMesh` provide closed test/demo fixtures.

Tests run with `node --test tests/engine.test.mjs` and cover parsing, progress, dimensions, topology, cavity winding, overlap refusal, formerly failing near-vertex slices, shared singular-plane recovery, exact duplicate cleanup, safe precision seams, refusal of real holes, vertical/slope planning, dense internal event exclusion, brief exterior cap/slope protection, manual overrides, final-height fitting, and end-to-end SLC encoding/decoding. `node tests/engine-benchmark.mjs` is an optional performance smoke with 100,016- and 480,016-triangle closed fixtures; it checks a 400-layer schedule without imposing a machine-dependent timing threshold.
